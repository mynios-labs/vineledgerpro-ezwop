Problem to fix

The Health Check says “0 policies” while Seller Hub shows active Payment, Fulfillment, and Return policies. That means our Sell Account API calls are not returning the real policies. The most likely causes are:

Wrong marketplace header on Sell Account endpoints. Missing or incorrect X-EBAY-C-MARKETPLACE-ID: EBAY_US.

Using a cached or Sandbox token. EBAY_ENV or token origin mismatch.

Missing scopes on the token when calling Sell Account. Must include https://api.ebay.com/oauth/api_scope/sell.account.

Filtering logic is wrong. We may be filtering out valid policies or only reading default flags.

Not passing Accept: application/json or sending an Accept-Language that hides policies.

Because the app believes there are no policies, the publish step either skips policy IDs or sends empty IDs. eBay then blocks the listing and we chase the wrong error.
